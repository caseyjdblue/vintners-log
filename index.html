Policy Platform

What We're Fighting For

These aren't just talking points—they're commitments. Here's where [CANDIDATE NAME] stands on the issues that matter most to families in the 41st District.

Healthcare for All Washingtonians

Every person in Washington deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare—regardless of their zip code or their income. [CANDIDATE NAME] will fight to protect and expand the ACA, advance a robust public option in Washington State, lower prescription drug costs by allowing Medicare negotiation, and ensure reproductive healthcare remains legal, accessible, and fully covered. Healthcare is not a privilege; it's a right.

Expand Public Option Lower Drug Costs Protect Reproductive Rights Mental Health Parity

Climate Action & Clean Energy

Washington has led the nation on climate—and we must go further. [CANDIDATE NAME] will champion a rapid transition to 100% clean electricity, accelerate EV infrastructure across our district, protect Puget Sound and our natural environment, hold polluters accountable, and create tens of thousands of good-paying green jobs right here in Washington. The climate crisis is an economic opportunity if we act now.

100% Clean Energy Protect Puget Sound Green Jobs Polluter Accountability

World-Class Public Education

A great education should be available to every child in the 41st District—not just those whose families can afford private schools or tutors. [CANDIDATE NAME] will fight for full funding of our public schools, competitive teacher pay, expanded early childhood education, and making community college and vocational training debt-free. Investing in education is investing in Washington's future.

Full School Funding Teacher Pay Early Childhood Ed Affordable College

Housing Affordability & Security

The housing crisis is squeezing families across our district. Young people can't afford to stay. Teachers and firefighters are being priced out. [CANDIDATE NAME] will push to expand the housing supply at all income levels, strengthen tenant protections, increase homelessness prevention funding, and cut red tape that delays affordable development. Everyone deserves a stable, safe place to call home.

Expand Housing Supply Tenant Protections Homelessness Prevention Affordable Development

An Economy That Works for Everyone

Washington's economy is thriving—but not everyone is sharing in that prosperity. [CANDIDATE NAME] will fight for a $20 minimum wage statewide, support the right to organize and bargain collectively, close corporate tax loopholes that ship profits offshore, invest in small businesses in our community, and make sure our tax code rewards work, not just wealth.

Living Wage Workers' Rights Small Business Support Fair Taxation

Community Safety & Gun Responsibility

Safe communities require both effective policing and investment in the root causes of crime. [CANDIDATE NAME] supports community-based public safety models, robust mental health crisis response, and evidence-based violence prevention programs. On gun safety: universal background checks, red flag laws, and a ban on assault-style weapons and high-capacity magazines. We can protect the Second Amendment and still save lives.

Universal Background Checks Red Flag Laws Mental Health Response Community Investment

Protecting Democracy & Voting Rights

Democracy only works when every voice is heard. [CANDIDATE NAME] will defend Washington's gold-standard mail ballot system, fight federal attacks on voting rights, support independent redistricting to end gerrymandering, increase transparency in campaign finance, and push for ranked-choice voting options that give voters more power. Government should work for people—not the other way around.

Protect Vote by Mail Campaign Finance Reform Anti-Gerrymandering Government Transparency
